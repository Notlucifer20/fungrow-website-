GOOD TO KNOW

Questions? Start here.

What is Funngro?

Funngro connects young people with project-based opportunities from companies and brands, helping them gain practical experience and develop skills.

What kind of projects can I explore?

Opportunities may include content creation, design, technology, research, surveys, social media and other brand-related activities. Availability can change.

Do I need experience to begin?

Not every project requires prior experience. Read each project brief to understand its requirements before applying.

How do I stay safe?

Use the official Funngro platform, read project instructions and involve a parent or guardian where required. Never share passwords, OTPs or sensitive personal information.
